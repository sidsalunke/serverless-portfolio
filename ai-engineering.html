Philosophy

Disciplined, not default.

AI adoption on my teams follows a rule: deterministic, mechanical checks get automated outright; Claude is reserved for work that genuinely needs judgement — architecture decisions, debugging, and design. That split keeps token spend sane and keeps engineers focused on the parts of the job that need a human brain. Guardrails are non-negotiable: no production credentials in reach of any AI tool, and a human approves every deployment.

Jira/Confluence → Teams Automation

Daily standup prep meant manually pulling status across Jira boards and Confluence pages. Built a bot that queries both APIs and posts a daily summary straight to Teams — the team gets one consistent summary instead of everyone assembling their own.

Unified Triage Board

Getting a full picture of defect health meant flipping between multiple Jira tabs, saved filters, and dashboards. Worked with Claude to design a single triage board pulling it all into one place, plus customised views that surface stale and at-risk defects specifically — instead of relying on someone remembering to check five different filters.
